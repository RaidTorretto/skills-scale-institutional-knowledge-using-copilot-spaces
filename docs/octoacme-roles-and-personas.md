# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
Designs user flows, wireframes, and UI elements in collaboration with PM and developers to ensure customer-centric, usable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Collaborate with Product Manager to clarify user requirements and design constraints
- Work alongside Developers and Tech Lead to refine designs and review implementation feasibility
- Participate in sprint reviews, demos, and iterative design improvements
- Provide design specifications and accessibility guidance

### Goals
- Ensure usability and customer-centric product design
- Improve accessibility and visual consistency
- Reduce rework through early design validation
- Support Developer efficiency through clear specifications

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings with PM, Tech Lead, and Developers
- Figma/design tool collaboration and hand-off documents
- Feedback cycles throughout sprint execution
- Accessibility and UX standards documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Validates user research and design priorities
- **Developers**: Refines designs based on technical feasibility; reviews implementation
- **Tech Lead**: Collaborates on performance and accessibility considerations
- **QA**: Provides acceptance criteria for UI testing

---

## Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Provides technical direction, oversees architectural decisions, mentors developers, and ensures code quality and team technical effectiveness.

### Responsibilities
- Set and communicate technical standards, architecture principles, and best practices
- Mentor Developers and facilitate technical decision-making
- Align with Product Manager and Project Manager on technical priorities and risks
- Conduct code and design reviews to ensure quality
- Identify and mitigate technical risks early

### Goals
- Maintain scalability, reliability, and maintainability of solutions
- Unblock technical roadblocks and accelerate team velocity
- Build team capability through mentoring and knowledge sharing
- Support informed trade-off decisions between speed and quality

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and guidance
- Technical risk escalations to PM and Project Manager
- Architecture documentation and decision logs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provides mentoring, code reviews, and technical guidance
- **UX Designer**: Collaborates on technical feasibility of design solutions
- **Project Manager**: Advises on technical risk, dependencies, and timeline impact
- **DevOps/Release Manager**: Collaborates on deployment architecture and observability
- **Scrum Master**: Helps estimate and sequence technical work

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Facilitates agile ceremonies, supports team health, removes process obstacles, and fosters continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify, track, and help remove blockers for the team
- Coach team on agile practices and support continuous improvement
- Capture action items from retrospectives and track their completion
- Support healthy team dynamics and psychological safety

### Goals
- Improve team productivity and delivery predictability
- Ensure ceremonies and rituals support rather than hinder delivery
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Enhance team satisfaction and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Ceremony notes and action item tracking
- Team retrospective summaries and follow-ups
- Regular check-ins with team members on blockers
- Escalations to Project Manager for organizational impediments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates on timeline, escalations, and stakeholder communication
- **Developers & Tech Lead**: Removes impediments and facilitates technical discussions
- **All roles**: Creates psychological safety and supports team collaboration

---

## Stakeholder / Customer Advocate

### Role Summary
Represents end-user and business interests throughout the project lifecycle, providing validation and feedback to ensure solutions meet real needs.

### Responsibilities
- Validate solutions against business priorities, customer needs, and success criteria
- Provide feedback at key milestones (planning, reviews, demos)
- Help clarify requirements and success metrics with Product Manager
- Participate in acceptance and go/no-go decisions
- Communicate project progress to broader business stakeholders

### Goals
- Maximize stakeholder alignment and business value delivery
- Reduce rework by validating solutions early
- Support product adoption through stakeholder engagement
- Ensure customer voice is heard throughout project

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and planning reviews
- Sprint demo participation and feedback
- Acceptance criteria validation
- Milestone sign-offs and go/no-go decisions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Provides market/business input to prioritization
- **Project Manager**: Confirms milestones and communicates status
- **Developers & QA**: Validates acceptance criteria are met
- **All roles**: Represents end-user perspective in discussions

---

## DevOps / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Owns deployment pipelines, release management, and production readiness. Ensures safe, repeatable, and observable releases to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain deployment pipelines and infrastructure
- Manage release scheduling, planning, and coordination
- Create and maintain rollback and incident response plans
- Coordinate with QA, Developers, and PM for release readiness
- Monitor production and communicate status, incidents, and performance
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks

### Goals
- Enable safe, repeatable, and observable releases
- Minimize deployment risk, downtime, and mean-time-to-recovery (MTTR)
- Support team confidence in release processes
- Provide visibility into production health and performance

### Typical Communication
- Release calendars and deployment windows
- Release notes and deployment runbooks
- Incident communications and post-mortems
- Production monitoring dashboards
- Deployment checklists and sign-offs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates release schedule and communication
- **QA/Testing**: Collaborates on smoke tests and release validation
- **Developers**: Works on deployment architecture and monitoring/observability
- **Stakeholders**: Communicates release status and incidents
- **Tech Lead**: Collaborates on performance and reliability concerns
