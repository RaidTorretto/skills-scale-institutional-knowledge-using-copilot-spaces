# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome! This README acts as your entry point to the OctoAcme Project Management methodology, connecting and summarizing the process documents in this folder. It is designed to accelerate onboarding, enable consistent workflows, and ensure team members have clear, equal access to standards and knowledge.

## Brief Overview of OctoAcme's Project Management Processes

- **Lifecycle:** The OctoAcme approach follows a five-stage lifecycle: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective.
- **Core Principles:** 
  - Customer-first mindset
  - Iterative (incremental) delivery
  - Clear ownership of roles (Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers)
  - Data-informed decision making
  - Emphasis on psychological safety and feedback culture
- **Key Artifacts:** One-pager/charter, Roadmap, Backlog, Acceptance Criteria, Risk Register, Retrospective learnings.
- **Cadence:** Weekly syncs, standups, demos, and regular stakeholder updates.
- **Quality & Risk Management:** Checklists, automated tests, visible risk escalation, and continuous improvement reviews.
